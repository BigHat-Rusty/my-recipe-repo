**Makes 1 8x8 inch pan**

---

### Dry Ingredients

- *100g (3/4 cup)* all-purpose flour
- *1g (1/4 tsp)* baking powder
- *4g (3/4 tsp)* salt

### Fats and Sugars

- *30g (2 Tbsp)* olive oil
- *115g (1 stick)* unsalted butter
- *150g (3/4 cup)* granulated sugar
- *50g (1/4 cup)* dark brown sugar

### Chocolate

- *100g (2/3 cup)* milk chocolate chips
- *50g (3/4 cup)* Dutch-processed cocoa powder
  - *65g* substitute with regular cocoa powder

### Other

- *5g (1 tsp)* vanilla extract
- *2* large eggs
- *85-100g (1/2 - 2/3 cup)* dark chocolate chips, chopped and sifted

### Cookware

- 8x8 inch Brownie Pan
- "Small" Saucepan
- Pan Spray
- Thermometer
- Whisk
- Rubber Spatula

---

### Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F (163°C).
1. Grease an 8x8-inch metal brownie pan with pan spray.
1. In a small bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.

### Melt and Combine Batter

1. Melt fats and sugars:
   - In a small saucepan over low heat, combine the olive oil, butter, white sugar, and brown sugar.
   - Stir until the butter is fully melted and the sugars are incorporated.
   - **Note**: The mixture will look sandy. That's expected.
1. Melt in the milk chocolate:
   - Remove from heat and add the milk chocolate chips, stirring until melted.
   - **Note**: The mixture will still appear grainy.
   - Transfer to a medium bowl and let cool for 3-5 minutes until warm but not hot.
1. Incorporate cocoa powder:
   - Whisk the Dutch-processed cocoa powder (or substitute regular cocoa powder) into the warm chocolate mixture until smooth and lump-free.
1. Add vanilla and eggs:
   - Stir in the vanilla extract and eggs, whisking thoroughly until fully combined.
   - Let the mixture rest for 10 minutes to allow the sugar to dissolve further.
   - After 10 minutes, whisk again for 30-60 seconds to ensure a silky batter.

### Finish Batter

1. Fold in dry ingredients:
   - Add the dry ingredient mixture to the wet ingredients and stir gently with a spatula until just combined.
   - **Note**: Avoid overmixing to prevent toughness.
1. Add dark chocolate chips:
   - Stir in the chopped dark chocolate chips until evenly distributed.
   - If desired, sift out any chocolate dust before adding to maintain contrast in the brownie.
1. Transfer to pan:
   - Pour the batter into the prepared 8x8-inch metal pan.
   - **Note**: Do not spread it with a spatula, this disrupts the brownie skin.
   - Firmly tap the pan on the counter multiple times to level out the batter.

### Bake

1. Place the pan in the preheated oven and bake for 35 minutes.
1. For best results, check doneness with an instant-read thermometer (202-205°F/95C).
1. Let the brownies cool in the pan for at least 60-90 minutes before cutting. This allows the texture and flavors to fully develop.
