# Chicken Fettuccine Alfredo

This version deviates from authentic Alfredo, similar to what is served in resurants today.  
It includes a few non-traditional ingredients, namely garlic and blackened chicken.

Ingredients and be multiplied to make larger servings.

### Main Ingredients

- *1 lb (16oz) (454g)* Chicken Breast
- *3 Cloves* Garlic
- *1+ cups* Parmigiano Reggiano (plus more for serving)
- *1 1/4 cups (300g)* Heavy Cream
- *3 tbsp* Flat-Leaf Italian Parsley
- *8 tbsp* Unsalted Butter

### As Needed

- Blackened Spice
- Olive Oil
- Salt
- Pepper
- All-Purpose Flower

### Cookware

- Knife
- Cutting Board
- Cheese Grater
- Gallon Ziplock Bag
- Mallet, for tenderizing meat
- Large Pot, for boiling Pasta
- Large Pan, for cooking Chicken
- Large Pan, for cooking the sauce
- Meat Thermometer

---

### Preperation

1. Measure Heavy Cream. Set aside in fridge.
1. Grate Parmigiano Reggiano. Set aside in fridge.
1. Cut apart Butter into 1 tbsp segments. Set aside in fridge.
1. Mince Garlic. Set aside.
1. Mince Italian Parsley. Set aside.
1. Weight pasta. Set aside the correct amount.
1. Prepare a pot of water with salt to boil the pasta in.

### Prepare the Chicken

1. Butterfly / Filet Chicken Breast.
1. Flatten the Chicken Breast pieces to about `3/8"` to `1/2″` thick.
   - Using a plastic bag and meat mallet is the easiest way to do this.
1. Season both sides of the Chicken Breast with Blackened Spice.
1. Dredge Chicken in Flour, then shake off excess.
1. Prepare a board/plate with foil to keep chicken warm.
1. Begin to boil the water.

### Cook the Chicken

1. Heat a stainless steel or cast iron pan to medium heat and wait 3 minutes.
1. Coat the pan with Olive Oil and add the Chicken Breast to the pan.
1. After 2 minutes add the butter.
1. The chicken should take about 3 minutes per side to cook.
   - Check for `165 F` with a Meat Thermometer.
1. Place chicken off to the side "tented" under foil.

### Cook Pasta and Sauce

1. Place pasta into boiling water and cook as directed.
1. Heat a large, clean pan to medium-low heat and add the butter and garlic.
1. Cook garlic until golden (about 2 minutes).
1. Add the cream and the cheese, then bring to a simmer.
   - The sauce will begin to thicken after a couple of minutes of simmering.
1. Once the pasta starts to bubble, reduce the heat to very low.
1. Lift pasta out of water and add to sauce. Mix or toss to coat.
   - If the pasta is too dry add some of the reserved pasta water, a bit at a time, to get a perfect consistency.
1. Add Parsley.
1. Add salt and pepper for taste.

### Final Product

1. Prepare individual plates with pasta, then add sliced chicken.
1. Serve with more grated Parmigiano Reggiano on the side. Enjoy!
