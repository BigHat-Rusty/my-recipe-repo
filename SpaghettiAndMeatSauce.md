**Serving Info**

### Ingredients

- *1lb/450g* 80/20 Ground Chuck
- *8oz/225g or 2 links* Hot Italian Pork Sausage
- *200g or 1 med-lrg* Onion, medium diced
- *20g or 4 cloves* Garlic, minced/pressed
- *1/2tsp* Dried Oregano
- *1/2tsp* Red Chili Flake
- *75g or 1/3c* Tomato Paste
- *1.5tsp* Coarse Salt
- *2tsp* Granulated Sugar
- *15g or 1Tbsp* Beef flavored Better than Bouillon
- *28oz/800g can* Crushed Tomatoes
- *28oz/800g* Water (enough Water to fill tomato can)
- *10g or 3Tbsp* Fresh Basil, chopped
  - OR *1g* Dried Basil
- *12oz/340g* Dried Spaghetti (broken in half)
- *50g or 1/2c* Grated Parmesan
  - plus extra for garnish

### Cookware

- Dutch Oven
- Meat Smasher
- Wooden Spoon
- Measuring Cup (for 1/2 cup)
- 2x Ramekins
- Glass (to weigh the spaghetti)

---

### Preperation

1. Dice onion thoroughly
1. Mince *20g or 4 cloves* Garlic
1. Combine into a Ramekin:
   - *75g or 1/3c* Tomato Paste
   - *1/2tsp* Oregano
   - *1/2tsp* Chile Flakes
   - *1g* Dried Basil (if applicable)
1. Combine into a Ramekin:
   - *1.5tsp* Salt
   - *2tsp* Sugar
   - *15g or 1Tbsp* Bouillon into a Ramekin
1. (If using fresh Basil, measure and set aside)
1. Measure *50g or 1/2c* Grated Parmesan into a Measuring Cup
1. Measure and break Dried Spaghetti
1. Open Crushed Tomatoes
1. Preheat oven to 425

### Cook the Meat

1. Heat the Dutch Oven over medium-high heat
1. Add a bit of oil, the ground beef, and sausage
1. Break the meat apart using a meat smasher
1. When the meat begins to brown and is well broken down, stir in the onion, garlic, and a pinch of salt
   - Make sure to scrape up any "meat fond" stuck to the bottom of the pot with a Wooden Spoon
1. Cover and cook for 5 minutes

### Build the Sauce

1. Add the combined Tomato Paste, Oregano, Chile Flakes, and Dried Basil
1. Stir and Cook for a minute or so until tomato paste is well incorporated
1. Add the combined Salt, Sugar, and Bouillon
1. Immediately add Crushed Tomatoes and Water
1. Stir and bring to a simmer

### Add the Noodles

1. Add the broken Dried Spaghetti
1. Gently stir and press down to submerge noodles
1. Simmer for about 60 seconds
1. Carefully stir noodles again to fully submerge noodles

### Oven Time

1. Transfer the pot to the oven to cook, uncovered for 16-18 min
   - Gently stir at 5 and 10 minutes into cooking
1. Taste for doneness and seasoning and adjust if necessary
1. Stir in Grated Parmesan
1. Garnish with additional Grated Parmesan
