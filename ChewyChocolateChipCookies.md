**Makes 8 1/2 cup cookies**

---

### Dry Ingredients

- *3g (1/2 tsp)* baking soda
- *3g (1/2 tsp)* baking powder
- *7g (1 1/4 tsp)* salt
- *330g (2 1/3 cup)* bread flour

### Butter and Sugars

- *230g (2 sticks)* unsalted butter, room temperature
- *100g (1/2 cup)* white sugar
- *200g (1 cup)* brown sugar

### Chocolate

- *225g (1 1/2 cup)* 70% cacao baking chocolate bars, chopped and sifted

### Other

- *5g (1 tsp)* vanilla extract
- *2* large eggs
- *1* egg yolk
- Flaky Salt

### Cookware

- Stand Mixer
- Rubber Spatula
- 4oz (1/2 cup) Portion Scoop
- Parchment Paper
- Whisk

---

### Preparation

1. Allow butter to come to room temperature.
1. Whisk together the bread flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.
1. Chop and sift chocolate bars. Set aside.

### Mix Butter and Sugar

1. In a mixer bowl, cream butter, white sugar, and brown sugar on high speed for 2-3 minutes
1. Scrape down sides to recombine ingredients.
1. Mix for 2-3 minutes, or until lighter in color and fluffy.

### Add Vanilla and Eggs

1. Add vanilla, eggs, and egg yolk.
1. Mix on low speed until just combined.

### Add Dry Ingredients

1. Add in dry ingredient mixture.
1. Mix on low speed for 1 minute.
1. Reserve 10-16 pieces of chopped chocolate for later.
1. Fold in chopped chocolate and mix briefly on low speed to distribute evenly.

### Scoop Dough

1. Prepare a parchment-lined sheet tray.
1. Scoop ~8 cookies:
   - Put a few pieces of reserved chocolate into a 4oz (1/2 cup) scoop, then scoop dough into balls.
1. Cover and freeze for at least 1 hour, preferably overnight.

### Bake

1. Place frozen dough balls on a baking sheet, allowing a lot of room in between each cookie.
   - Note: 4 at a time is recommended.
1. Sprinkle with flaky salt, and bake for 18-19 minutes at 375°F (190°C) until edges are golden and centers are soft.
1. Let cool slightly before serving.
